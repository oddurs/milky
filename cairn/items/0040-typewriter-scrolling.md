Keeps the caret's line at a fixed height in the window rather than letting it
walk to the bottom edge. Pairs with focus mode; iA Writer and Bear both have it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The caret's line holds a configurable height, default centred
- [ ] Only while typing — clicking and scrolling stay free
- [ ] Does not fight the existing scroll-to-selection behaviour
